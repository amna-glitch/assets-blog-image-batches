How it looks on your articles page

Twelve real titles in a replica of your listing card, which shows only the top 56% of each image. Every style keeps its faces, cards and numbers in that band.

Rules that keep it varied

  1. The article type picks the style; never choose by taste per post.
  2. No two neighbouring cards use the same style.
  3. Rotate the field colour inside a style (pale blue, pale aqua, deep blue).
  4. Cards show specific, readable content, never placeholder bars.
  5. No corner checkmark and no title text on the image. No prices or figures until your team confirms them.

Before using these live

The photos in styles 1, 3 and 6 are crops of your existing images, so some old floating cards still show at the edges. Fresh photos without built-in UI will look cleaner. Renders use Inter in place of Sequel Sans.
