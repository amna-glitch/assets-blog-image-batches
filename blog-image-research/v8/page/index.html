10 photo covers, 10 different people

Five young women and five young men of different races and hair types, generated in ChatGPT from the prompts and placed into 10 different layouts. All use the same see-through gradient and frosted glass as the 72 word-free covers. No words, no checkmarks, no old floating tiles.

The source photos

ChatGPT returned all ten as one collage. I cut it into ten photos and matched each to a layout that suits its size: the two widest shots run full-width (P03, P09), and the tall ones sit in the arch (P01) and the phone (P06).

Note: cut from one collage, each photo is small (about 260–770 px wide). They look fine in the framed layouts, but the full-width covers (P03, P09) are a little soft. For final use, regenerate those two people as single, full-size images.

Picks from your current library

I scanned all 1,921 readable images in your library and shortlisted 16 young people who look clearly different from the rest: 8 women and 8 men, including braids, locs, natural coily and curly hair, darker skin tones, one East Asian woman and one East Asian man. The red dot marks each face.

What the scan found: the library has no light-skinned, red- or blonde-haired people, no one in a hijab and no shaved heads. Almost everyone else has medium or olive skin and dark hair, which is why the covers feel samey. These 16 are full-size images, so they hold up as full-width covers. Pair them with the 10 ChatGPT people for a varied set.
