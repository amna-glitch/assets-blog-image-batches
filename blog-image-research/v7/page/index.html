Rules every cover follows

  • No words or letters. Digits appear only as part of an object, such as a clock face, a podium or a code chip.
  • No prices, no currency signs and no question marks. The coin icon is a plain coin stack.
  • No third-party logos. EHRs are shown as a generic monitor.
  • Brand blues, teal and cyan, with coral only for a problem signal (a denial, a ringing phone).
  • Every title is a real article from your site.
